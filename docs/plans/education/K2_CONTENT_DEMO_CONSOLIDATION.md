# Per-screen Learn guidance for the consolidated demo

**Authored:** 2026-09-13 · **status: authored**

These entries describe the current controller and page actions. They are display guidance,
not new constitutional rules or promises of content that has not been published.

### 1. `economy/work` — My work and hiring
**learn:** Review your applications and offers, or manage hiring for an organization you represent. An offer becomes an agreement only through the applicant's acceptance and the organization's countersignature.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose your side of the conversation | My applications shows opportunities you applied for. Hiring shows the organizations you represent; select an organization and then a posting to review its applicants. | — |
| 2 | Publish or review work | Employers can publish a posting with its terms and review applications. To find an opportunity as a worker, open Find work from this page. Lists have Previous and Next links for browsing in manageable portions. | — |
| 3 | Read the offer before accepting | An employer can make an offer or decline an application. The applicant can accept the offered terms or withdraw; accepting does not sign on behalf of the employer. | — |
| 4 | Finish the agreement | After acceptance, the employer countersigns to record the hire. Open the linked agreement to read the terms and signatures. Only the relevant applicant or organization representative can perform their part. | — |

> **The why:** both parties consent to the same terms. Browsing or applying does not silently create an employment agreement.

### 2. `economy/help` — Give and find help
**learn:** Ask for help or offer it to someone else. Requests can begin as private drafts; publishing makes an open request visible to people who can respond.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find the requests you need | Browse open public requests, My requests, or My offers. Use Previous and Next to continue through the list. | — |
| 2 | Write a clear request | Give it a title and describe what you need. Save a private draft if you are not ready for responses, or publish it to invite offers of help. | — |
| 3 | Open a request to take part | Read the details before offering help. The requester reviews offers privately and chooses a helper; other helpers cannot read your offer. | — |
| 4 | Check participation messages | If you need an economic account before posting or responding, the page explains that next step. Asking for or offering help does not transfer money or create an automatic fee. | — |

### 3. `economy/help-detail` — A help request
**learn:** Keep one request and its offers together. The requester chooses a helper and closes the request when it is resolved or no longer needed.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check whether the request is open | A private draft is visible to its requester. Publishing an open draft invites offers; a matched request already has a selected helper. | — |
| 2 | Offer help privately | On an open public request, describe the help you can offer. The requester sees the offers; each helper sees only their own response. | — |
| 3 | Choose or withdraw an offer | The requester can accept one offer. A helper can withdraw their own offer; if the selected helper withdraws, the request opens again. | — |
| 4 | Finish the request | The requester can mark it resolved or withdraw it. The request and response history remain available to the people allowed to read them. | — |

### 4. `rooms/directory` — Live rooms
**learn:** Find public chambers, committee meetings, and hearings by place. Room access follows the institution and meeting you open; browsing the directory does not join a call.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose a place | Use the place navigation to explore the jurisdiction tree, including places away from your current location. The directory follows the selected place. | — |
| 2 | Choose the kind of meeting | Browse chambers, committees, or court hearings. Open a listed room to see its floor and meeting context; committee entries use the available meeting link. | — |
| 3 | Find your board rooms | Board rooms appear for boards where you currently hold a seat. Their private membership rules still apply when you enter. | — |
| 4 | Continue the conversation | Open Messages and groups for private conversations. Joining a room's audio or video is a separate action from reading its public page. | — |

### 5. `learn/video-library` — Video library
**learn:** Choose a film, then choose its audio and subtitles. The library lists available catalog entries; actual playback also needs the instance's media host and tracks to be available.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose a film | Select a title from the library to load it in the player above. Its audio and caption counts show the catalog's language choices. | — |
| 2 | Choose your languages | Link audio and subtitles to use one language for both, or unlink them to choose each separately. You can turn captions off. The player remembers your choices in this browser. | — |
| 3 | Watch the playback status | Use play, pause, and the timeline when media is available. Loading, buffering, and playback failures are shown in the player; an unavailable track has a retry action. | — |
| 4 | Continue learning | Open Full lessons in Learn for written lessons and knowledge checks. A catalog entry or placeholder does not mean a film has already been published for every lesson. | — |
