### 1. `legislature/institution-acts` — Institution acts
**learn:** Propose the institution first; appointments and elections follow its adopted charter.

| # | do | detail | cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose an institution action | The selected legislature supplies the jurisdiction and the responsible offices. Governor and judge nominations happen after creation. | F-LEG-014 · F-LEG-015 · F-LEG-016 · F-LEG-017 · F-LEG-018 · F-LEG-019 |
| 2 | Read and vote on the proposal | Use the public vote shown with the act. The meter comes from the vote engine; the Speaker votes only to break an eligible tie. | F-LEG-004 · F-SPK-004 |
| 3 | Complete constituent consent | Executive and court conversions show each constituent jurisdiction’s decision. Open a participating legislature to file its own consent vote. | F-LEG-015 · F-LEG-018 |

> **The why:** The proposal, public decision and resulting institution remain linked; creating an office does not grant its authority to the proposer.
