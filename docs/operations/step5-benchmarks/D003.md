## One bounded next recommendation

**Inspect whether training's disbursement preparation can be completed before the group's first ledger-lock acquisition, while retaining the atomic group. Implement only if that can be done narrowly and validated.**

Concrete reason: `TrainingStipendService.php:199–208` now calls `creditManyFromTreasury` while mint's transaction-scoped lock is already held. `AccountService.php:221` then constructs legs and wallet sums, and `LedgerService.php:82–106` validates/prepares UUIDs, canonical payloads and treasury deltas for the second posting inside that held-lock interval. The earlier preparation optimization is therefore outside the second reentrant acquisition but no longer outside the group's first acquisition. The nested credit costs 4.78 ms including its ledger and wallet work; the unmeasured preparation share must not be presented as that entire cost or as a promised speedup.

A narrow preparation/execution separation could preserve both issuance and stipend entry groups, fresh head reads after lock acquisition, exact canonical hashes, wallet and treasury mutations, authority checks, once-only behavior, the outer atomic rollback and owned durable commit. Do not remove savepoints or weaken locks/durability as a shortcut. Avoid a broad ledger rewrite or unrelated payment-path behavior change. Include preparation and full group/commit measurement, concurrency, rollback, retakes, and exact balance/hash tests. If safe preparation cannot be separated without disproportionate scope, return that completed disposition instead of inventing work; then the next natural Phase 7 benchmark should guide further changes.
